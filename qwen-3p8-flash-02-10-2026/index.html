WHAT THIS MACHINE IS

the composition

A seeded engine decides everything, deterministically: a chain of related keys and modes, tempos, diatonic progressions drawn from per-mode pools with suspensions and a borrowed minor iv, seventh chords at your discretion of probability. Each 64-bar movement gets a motif — a rhythm cell and a contour in diatonic steps, which is why inversion, retrograde and augmentation can never leave the key. Melodies are harmonized bar by bar: strong beats snap to chord tones, weak beats may pass through. Pads are voiced by brute-force nearest-octave voice leading against the previous chord. Six named sections per movement (Threshold, Ground, Clearing, Drift, Signal, Horizon) shape an energy curve that gates every voice, and the machine writes program notes about what it is doing.

the sound

No audio files exist anywhere in this project. The reverb is a stereo impulse response generated at startup from smoothed noise with pre-delay and early reflections. Detuned saw pads breathe under a shared filter LFO; the bass is sine + envelope-swept saw; plucks are triangle/saw through a falling lowpass; the lead is an FM flute with delayed vibrato and portamento; chimes are two-operator FM with inharmonic ratios; drums are pitch-dive sines and band-passed noise; wind and risers are the same noise, filtered. Everything is scheduled with sample-accurate Web Audio automation, ~7 instruments, ping-pong delay locked to the piece's own tempo.

No frameworks. No build step. No network calls after load. The only runtime dependencies are mathematics and the AudioContext.
