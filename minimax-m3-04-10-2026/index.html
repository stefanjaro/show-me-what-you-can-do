How to play

PROTOGAIA is an artificial life simulator. Drop a few creatures into a tiny pocket of land and watch what happens over thousands of generations.

  • Paint the world with food, plants, water, walls or hot spots using the brushes on the left.
  • Follow a specific creature to see its daily life.
  • Control a creature by pressing keys (WASD/Arrows + Space).
  • Observe free-roaming with the mouse.
  • The tree and graphs tabs show long-term evolution.
  • The chronicle records every speciation and extinction in writing.

There is no goal. The world is yours to perturb — or to leave alone.

Tips

  • Press R to spawn a starter creature at the cursor.
  • Press F to add food under the cursor.
  • Press 1–9 to switch brushes from the keyboard.
  • Press Space to pause, . to step a single frame.
  • Press + / - to cycle simulation speed.

About

Every line of this world — the physics, the brains, the genomes, the music — is written from scratch. No libraries, no assets, no build step. Open js/ in the repo to read the source.
