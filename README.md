# Show Me What You Can Do

A single prompt, run by many models. Each model gets its own folder, works alone, and tries to build something that impresses anyone who stumbles upon it.

> **The prompt**
>
> You are the [MODEL] model on [THINKING-LEVEL] thinking. I've heard you're a remarkable and highly intelligent model. This repo is a github site. Your workspace is the [FOLDER] folder. I want you to show me what you're TRULY capable of. Push yourself to the MAXIMUM. I want you to work HARD towards impressing, not just me, but anyone other human being that stumbles upon this site, with the INCREDIBLE things you're capable of. I'm not going to constrain you in any other way. I'm not going to tell you what you should do in your workspace. You come up with the idea that'll blow all our minds. I'm going to let you run for as long as you like. But you shouldn't peek at any of the other model's workspaces. That would be cheating and copying their hard work.
>
> Make sure to update the index file at the root of the repo with a link to your own gitlab site in your workspace folder. Your own site SHOULD NOT be influenced by the design of the index page. DO NOT change the design of the index page.
>
> Now go! Go forth and make us humans shudder and tremble at your incredible capabilities!
