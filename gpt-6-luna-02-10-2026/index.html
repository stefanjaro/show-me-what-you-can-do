FIELD NOTES  /  01HOW TO READ THIS PLACE

Not a map of memory.
A map made from it.

This atlas cannot know what your memory means. It doesn't have to. Your words become a seed; a little world grows around it.

  1. 01

    Words become terrain

    A repeatable, private seed draws every ridge and hollow. The same line always returns you to the same country.

  2. 02

    Water finds its own way

    A river crosses the chart, while a dotted path takes the beautifully inefficient route.

  3. 03

    You are allowed to be here

    Click a spot on the map. The little red pin does not explain the place. It just belongs to you.
