Writing scenes

Scenes are signed distance functions: a single expression in x, y, z, t (plus u, v for mouse). Negative means inside, positive outside, zero is the surface.

Operators

+ - * / ^ % and parentheses. ^ is power.

Functions

sin cos tan asin acos atan atan2 sinh cosh tanh exp log sqrt abs floor ceil round min max pow mod fract clamp mix step smoothstep

len(x,y,z), dot(ax,ay,az,bx,by,bz), noise(x,y,z) (value noise), fbm(x,y,z).

Built-in shapes

sphere(x,y,z,r), box(x,y,z,sx,sy,sz), torus(x,y,z,R,r), cylinder(x,y,z,r,h), plane(x,y,z,nx,ny,nz,d).

Fractals

mandelbulb(x,y,z), menger(x,y,z), gyroid(x,y,z,scale), julia(x,y,z).

Combining

Union: min(a,b). Intersection: max(a,b). Subtraction: max(a,-b). Smooth union: smin(a,b,k). Repetition: mod(x, period)-period*0.5.

Examples

len(x,y,z)-1                          // sphere
smin(sphere(x,y,z,0.9), box(x,y-0.6,z,0.5,0.5,0.5), 0.3)   // blobby union
mandelbulb(x,y,z)                     // mandelbulb fractal
gyroid(x,y,z,1.2)+0.1*sin(t*2)       // animated gyroid
max(len(x,y,z)-1.2, - (len(x,y,z)-0.9))  // hollow sphere
noise(x*3,y*3,z*3)*0.3 + len(x,y,z)-1     // bumpy sphere
