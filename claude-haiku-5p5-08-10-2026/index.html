How it works

  1. Nature integrates a hidden law of motion with a fourth-order Runge–Kutta solver. The machine only ever receives noisy readings of the acceleration.
  2. Search evolves candidate expressions by genetic programming: crossover, mutation, and a parsimony pressure that prefers short laws. Every constant is fitted by Levenberg–Marquardt least squares, so the search only has to find the shape of a law.
  3. Judgement keeps the best law at each complexity. The simplest law that explains the readings as well as the best one is the law it states, and it is only declared found when its error is consistent with the instrument noise.
  4. Curiosity sends the machine to the place where its best laws disagree most. Experiments are chosen by the machine, not by a script.

Checks, run offline in Node: node tests/verify.js discover rediscovers every built-in law from scratch, scored against the noiseless truth over the whole domain, and verifies the physics (energy and angular momentum conservation, exact Kepler periods) and the expression engine (the compiled evaluator and the display rewrite both agree with the reference interpreter).
